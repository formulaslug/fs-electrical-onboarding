Definitions for words I do not know

Op Amps (Operational Amplifier)

A voltage amplifying device that performs various operations like addition, subtraction, integration, etc.. It’s an IC!!!
You can have voltage- feedback op amp or current-feedback op amp.

See Op Amp Research
BSPD (Brake System Plausibility Device)


Comparator


LVMS (Low Voltage Master Switch)
A single switch that controls the low voltage power supply to the car’s systems, ideally eliminates dependence on LV battery
Actuator
Something that turns energy into physical movement (in our case it can take electrical current and turn it into mechanical output to push, pull, lift, etc.)
Off Set Voltage


Input bias Current
Although in theory, there's no current going into an op amp, in reality, there is a very teeny tiny current going in that can complicate things
Slew rate
Describes how voltage cannot change instantaneously and gradually works its way up or down. This can impact delays and time, especially if you’re working with AC currents because those need to change really really quickly.
“Feedback” (for circuits)
The ability of the output to be able to influence the input
Throttle




About BSPD:
Non programmable circuit that opens shutdown circuit when u brake too hard and theres crazy stuff happening with the motor
Shutdown circuit stays open until BSPD resets itself if everything is chill for 10 seconds
If weird stuff happens for more than 500 ms (braking, voltage, current issues), shutdown circuit MUST open -> keeps everything and everyone safe
BSPD must be supplied from LVMS
