# FS Electrical Onboarding

This repo contains onboarding projects for each electrical subsystem:

- Low Voltage (LV)
- Power Electronics (PE)
- Firmware (FW)

You are encouraged to complete whichever projects you find interesting. Check
out the individual READMEs in each subsystem's folder for the projects you're
interested in!

You must complete one onboarding project to join the team. Our goal is to get to
know you, not to grade your skills or abilities. We want to see that you're
excited and interested, not that you know everything or finish fastest! To make
team roster selection easier for us, you'll only officially be assigned to one
specific subsystem within the Electrical team. But once you've joined, you're
encouraged to contribute and participate in as many ways as you're excited to
and have bandwidth for.
